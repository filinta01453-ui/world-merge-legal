Worlds Merge: Puzzle & Town

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · Terms of Service

This Privacy Policy explains what information Worlds Merge: Puzzle & Town (the “Game”, Android package com.sitora.ai) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. “We”, “us” and “our” mean the developer of the Game. By installing or playing the Game you confirm that you have read this policy.

In short: We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or exact location. The Game collects limited technical and gameplay data to run, to show ads, to measure how the Game is used, to send optional notifications and to back up your progress. Ads are shown by Appodeal and its partners, who may use your device advertising ID.

1. Information we and our service providers collect

1.1 Game progress and settings (stored on your device)

Your level progress, coins, gems, energy, items, settings and tutorial status are stored locally on your device. This data stays on your device unless you use cloud save (section 1.5).

1.2 Advertising data (Appodeal)

The Game shows rewarded and interstitial ads through the Appodeal mobile advertising platform and its ad network partners. To deliver and measure ads, Appodeal and its partners may collect your Android advertising ID (AAID), IP address, device and operating system information, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about ads you view or interact with. This is why the Game declares the AD_ID permission. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). See the Appodeal Privacy Policy.

1.3 Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is used, for example how many players return after one or seven days, and where players leave the tutorial. Firebase may collect an app instance ID, device model, operating system version, language, approximate country, and in-game events (such as tutorial steps). We do not use analytics to identify you personally. See Firebase privacy information.

1.4 Push notifications and in-game inbox

If you allow notifications, the Game uses Firebase Cloud Messaging and a small server operated by us (hosted on Cloudflare Workers) to deliver inbox messages and rewards. For this the Game sends our server a randomly generated player ID and access token, your invite code, your device’s push token and the state of inbox messages (read or claimed). These identifiers are random and are not linked to your name or email address. You can turn notifications off at any time in your Android settings. The Game can also schedule local reminders (for example when your energy is full) that never leave your device.

1.5 Google Play Games and cloud save

Signing in with Google Play Games is optional and is used for cloud save (“Saved Games”). If you sign in, your game progress is stored by Google in your Google account so you can restore it on another device. Google processes your Play Games profile and saved data under the Google Privacy Policy. We receive only the saved game data needed to restore your progress. You can delete saved games from the Play Games app settings.

1.6 Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card or billing details. We receive confirmation that a purchase was completed so we can deliver the item.

1.7 Network time check

To keep energy and timed rewards fair, the Game checks the current time by making simple web requests to public servers (such as Google or Cloudflare). These servers can see your IP address as with any internet request. No gameplay data is sent in these requests.

1.8 Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or precise GPS location.

2. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and improve the Game, including saving and restoring progress.
  • To show and measure ads, which help keep the Game free.
  • To measure usage, fix bugs and balance gameplay (analytics).
  • To deliver notifications, inbox messages and rewards.
  • To process purchases and prevent fraud, cheating and abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Legal bases (EEA, UK)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: consent (for personalized advertising, advertising ID use and optional notifications), performance of a contract (providing the Game and your purchases), and our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, basic analytics and improving the Game). When you first open the Game in the EEA, UK or other regions that require it, you are shown a consent screen for ads. You can withdraw consent at any time by resetting your advertising ID in Android settings, or by contacting us.

6. Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app or its data. Server-side inbox identifiers are kept while the Game is in use and are removed on request or when they are inactive for a long period. Analytics data is retained by Firebase according to its standard retention settings. Saved games remain in your Google account until you delete them.

  • Notifications: disable them in Android settings at any time.
  • Cloud save: sign out of Play Games or delete saved games in Play Games settings.
  • Delete data: uninstalling the Game removes data stored on your device. To ask us to delete server-side data linked to your player ID, email us at the address below.
  • Privacy rights: depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, Turkey under KVKK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to object to processing or withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, contact us. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell personal information for money. Sharing of advertising identifiers for advertising purposes may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under some state laws. You can opt out by resetting or limiting your advertising ID in Android settings or by contacting us.
